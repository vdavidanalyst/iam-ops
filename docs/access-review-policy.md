# Access Review Policy — IAM-Ops

## Review Cadence
Quarterly, or triggered ad-hoc after a security incident.

## Scope
All active user accounts and their group-based access assignments.

## Flagging Criteria
- Deactivated users who still retain active group assignments
- Users inactive 30+ days who still have active access
- Users who have never logged in but retain active access (e.g., abandoned onboarding)

## Sign-off Process
- Department Manager reviews flagged users within their department
- Security Lead reviews cross-departmental and IT Admin-level flags
- Access is revoked or re-justified within 5 business days of being flagged

## Escalation
Flags involving Finance-Sensitive or IT Admin group membership are treated as higher priority given SOX/compliance relevance and are escalated for same-week review.
